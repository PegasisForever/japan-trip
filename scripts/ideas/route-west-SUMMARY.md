## Trade-offs compared with the current Tohoku route
- Gain: Kyoto, Nara, Osaka food, Nagoya car museums, Ghibli Park, more anime places (Uji, Haruhi, Tamako Market, Yuru Camp, Medalist), the Suzuka F1 track (EV kart).
- Lose: Zao ski and snow monsters, Hakodate by Shinkansen, and 1–3 Hokkaido days (A and B have only about 1.5 ski days).
- Cost: similar or a little more. No JR pass pays off. Buy single tickets.
- Kansai has less snow than Tohoku: snow at Kyoto temples is luck.
- Flights home: the new Air Canada Sapporo → Vancouver nonstop (Tue/Fri/Sun, runs to Mar 26, 2027) and a Spring Airlines Sapporo → Shanghai nonstop let both travellers leave from Sapporo.
